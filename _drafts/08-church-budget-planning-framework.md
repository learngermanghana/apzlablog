Healthy budgets are strategic documents, not just spreadsheets.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Framework pillars
- Mission-priority funding
- Scenario planning for giving changes
- Quarterly variance reviews
- Transparent reporting to leaders

With Apzla reporting, churches can monitor budget health with less manual effort.
