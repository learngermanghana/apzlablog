Bad data causes missed follow-up, weak reports, and volunteer confusion.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Monthly cleanup checklist
- Merge duplicate profiles.
- Standardize phone and address fields.
- Archive inactive households.
- Validate ministry assignments.
- Update consent and communication preferences.

A clean database increases team confidence and improves ministry execution.
