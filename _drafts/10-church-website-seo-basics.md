SEO helps people find your church when they search for community and support.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Quick wins
- Optimize title tags and meta descriptions.
- Add location keywords naturally.
- Improve page speed and mobile usability.
- Publish FAQ sections for key ministries.
- Keep Google Business Profile updated.

Better search visibility supports both outreach and attendance growth.
