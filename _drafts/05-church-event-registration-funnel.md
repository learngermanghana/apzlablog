Many churches promote events but skip conversion strategy.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## High-performing event funnel
1. Landing page with one clear CTA.
2. Simple registration form.
3. Automated reminder sequence.
4. Day-of event check-in workflow.
5. Post-event follow-up and next-step invite.

When connected in Apzla, this funnel helps teams turn event interest into ongoing engagement.
