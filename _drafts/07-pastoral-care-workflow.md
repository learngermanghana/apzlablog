Pastoral care becomes sustainable when the process is clear and shared.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Core workflow stages
- Intake and prioritization
- Assignment to care owners
- Follow-up deadlines
- Outcome notes and next steps
- Escalation for urgent cases

Apzla helps care teams document responsibly while protecting sensitive information.
