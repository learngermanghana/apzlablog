A reliable **church visitor follow-up system** is one of the fastest ways to improve retention and long-term engagement.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Why follow-up drives church growth
- Guests decide quickly whether they will return.
- Consistent communication builds trust.
- Pastoral teams avoid missed connections.

## 7-step workflow churches can automate
1. Capture guest details digitally.
2. Send a same-day welcome text.
3. Trigger a 3-email welcome sequence.
4. Assign a care follow-up owner.
5. Invite the guest to a next-step event.
6. Track attendance and responses.
7. Review conversion metrics monthly.

With Apzla, each step can run from one dashboard so your team spends less time on admin and more time on people.
