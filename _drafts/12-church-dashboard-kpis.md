Good leadership needs clear, timely, and actionable numbers.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## KPI categories
- Attendance and retention trends
- Giving growth and recurring donation rate
- Small group participation
- Volunteer fulfillment rate
- Care request completion and response times

Apzla dashboards help leadership teams connect data to pastoral action.
