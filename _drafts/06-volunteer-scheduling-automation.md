Volunteer burnout is often a scheduling problem, not a commitment problem.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Automation ideas
- Role-based volunteer pools
- Auto-reminders 72 and 24 hours before service
- One-click shift swaps
- Instant backup requests when declines happen

Apzla gives ministry leads one place to coordinate and communicate schedules.
