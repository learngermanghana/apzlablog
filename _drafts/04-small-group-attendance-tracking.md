Small groups are core to pastoral care, but only if leaders can see trends early.

For Apzla's target market, local context matters: churches in **Ghana and across Africa** often manage fast-growing congregations, mobile-first communication, and multi-campus coordination with limited admin teams.

## Metrics to track monthly
- First-time attendee count
- 4-week retention rate
- Leader-to-member ratio
- Prayer and care follow-up completion

Apzla centralizes attendance and care notes so leaders can intervene before people disengage.
