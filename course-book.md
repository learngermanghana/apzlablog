# Apzla Features

Apzla brings every part of church administration into one secure platform.

- **Member Directory**: Centralized profiles with family relationships, notes, and care history.
- **Attendance & Groups**: Track services, small groups, and volunteer teams in one place.
- **Giving & Stewardship**: Online donations, batch entry, and year-end reporting.
- **Communication Hub**: Email and SMS messaging with segmented lists.
- **Event Management**: Registrations, check-ins, and calendar coordination.
- **Reports & Insights**: Real-time dashboards for leadership and staff.
