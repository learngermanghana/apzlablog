# Apzla Features

Apzla gives church teams one place to manage people, stewardship, and ministry workflows.

## Core Platform Capabilities

- **Member Directory** → Unified profiles for members, visitors, and volunteers
- **Giving & Stewardship** → Track contributions, pledges, and fund allocations with transparency
- **Groups & Ministries** → Organize teams, small groups, and attendance in real time
- **Events & Services** → Schedule services, registrations, and automated reminders
- **Communication Hub** → Email and SMS outreach with targeted segmentation
- **Follow-up & Care** → Automate next steps for guests and pastoral care

---

## Built for Church Operations

- Secure, role-based access for staff and volunteers
- Mobile-friendly experience for on-the-go ministry
- Reports for attendance, giving trends, and engagement
- Easy data migration from spreadsheets or legacy tools
