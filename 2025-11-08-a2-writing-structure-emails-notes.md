A warm welcome matters, but consistency turns first-time guests into connected members. A follow-up journey ensures no one slips through the cracks after their first visit.

---

## 1. Capture guest details immediately
Make it easy for guests to share their information—digital or physical.

**Best practices**
- Use simple forms with only key fields
- Offer QR codes for mobile connection cards
- Create guest profiles automatically in your member directory

---

## 2. Send a same-day thank-you message
A quick message helps guests feel seen and appreciated.

**Sample message**
> Hi [Name], it was great meeting you at church today. We’re grateful you joined us and would love to stay connected.

---

## 3. Assign a follow-up owner
Accountability keeps the process moving.

**What to assign**
- A task for a ministry leader or care volunteer
- A due date for a personal check-in
- Notes from the first visit to personalize the outreach

---

## 4. Invite guests to a next step
Make the next step clear and easy to join.

**Options to include**
- Newcomer gatherings
- Small group introductions
- Volunteer opportunities

---

## 5. Track outcomes and improve
Every guest journey should be measurable.

**Track metrics like**
- Response rate to the first message
- Attendance at a next step event
- Repeat visits within 30 days
